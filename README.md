# Awesome-Enterprise-Resource-Planning-ERP

## Top Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Business Process Automation, Modular ERP Frameworks & Self-Hosted Operations*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)**. These systems integrate core business functions—accounting, inventory, manufacturing, CRM, and supply chain—into a unified platform, enabling organizations to operate from a single source of truth.



**Examples** include Dynamics 365, SAP S/4HANA Cloud, Oracle Fusion Cloud, Workday, NetSuite, Epicor ERP, Sage X3, Acumatica, Unit4, and Odoo (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom module development, and transparent business logic—ideal for organizations that prioritize data sovereignty, avoid per-user licensing costs, and require auditable systems. The open-source ERP landscape is anchored by **Odoo Community** (largest module ecosystem), **ERPNext** (modern full-suite alternative), and **Apache OFBiz** (enterprise Java framework), with strong contenders in **Tryton** (modular manufacturing), **metasfresh** (distribution), and **Dolibarr** (simplicity for SMBs).



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Microsoft Dynamics 365](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365)**  

  Microsoft's suite of business applications covering ERP, CRM, and supply chain, tightly integrated with Office 365 and Azure. Offers Finance, Supply Chain Management, and Business Central tiers for different organization sizes.



- **[SAP S/4HANA Cloud](https://www.sap.com/products/erp/s4hana.html)**  

  SAP's flagship intelligent ERP for large enterprises, running on the in-memory HANA database. Provides real-time analytics, AI integration, and industry-specific processes. Enterprise-tier pricing with significant implementation complexity.



- **[Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP](https://www.oracle.com/erp/)**  

  Oracle's cloud ERP suite with modules for financials, procurement, project management, and risk. Strong for organizations already invested in Oracle's database and cloud infrastructure.



- **[Workday](https://www.workday.com/)**  

  Cloud-native platform specializing in HCM (Human Capital Management) and Financial Management. Known for strong user experience, analytics, and continuous updates without version upgrades.



- **[Infor CloudSuite](https://www.infor.com/products/cloudsuite)**  

  Industry-specific ERP suites for manufacturing, healthcare, retail, and other verticals. Cloud-deployed with Infor OS for integration and AI services.



- **[NetSuite](https://www.netsuite.com/)**  

  Oracle's cloud ERP for mid-market and growing businesses. Unified platform covering ERP, CRM, e-commerce, and professional services automation. Subscription-based per-user pricing.



- **[Epicor ERP](https://www.epicor.com/)**  

  ERP solutions tailored for manufacturing, distribution, retail, and service industries. Offers both cloud and on-premise deployment options with deep industry functionality.



- **[Sage X3](https://www.sage.com/en-us/products/sage-x3/)**  

  ERP for mid-sized businesses with strong manufacturing, distribution, and accounting capabilities. Available via cloud or on-premise with regional localization support.



- **[Acumatica](https://www.acumatica.com/)**  

  Cloud ERP with consumption-based pricing (no per-user fees) and a modular architecture. Strong for SMBs and mid-market companies seeking flexible deployment options.



- **[Unit4](https://www.unit4.com/)**  

  ERP focused on service organizations, professional services, and public sector. Known for strong project accounting and people-centric workflows.



- **[Odoo Enterprise](https://www.odoo.com/)**  

  Commercial tier of Odoo with full accounting, native mobile apps, Odoo Studio (no-code customization), and official support. Per-user subscription pricing with all apps included .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Odoo Community](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  The most widely adopted open-source ERP with 80+ business applications covering CRM, sales, inventory, manufacturing, and basic invoicing. The Community edition is free and self-hosted; accounting is limited compared to Enterprise . LGPL-3.0 licensed. Best for SMBs that need broad functional coverage with a massive third-party module ecosystem.



- **[ERPNext (Frappe)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Modern, full-suite open-source ERP built on the Frappe Framework (Python/JavaScript with MariaDB). Covers accounting, inventory, manufacturing, CRM, HR, and projects with a clean web UI. The Frappe framework provides a complete admin interface, REST API, and role-based permissions out of the box . GPL-3.0 licensed. Best for organizations that want a complete, modern alternative to Odoo with active development.



- **[Apache OFBiz](https://github.com/apache/ofbiz-framework)**  

  Apache 2.0 business application suite and Java framework for developer-led enterprise customization. Modules span accounting, CRM, order management, e-commerce, warehousing, manufacturing, and MRP . Best for enterprises with Java skills that want to build custom enterprise applications on a solid framework foundation.



- **[Tryton](https://github.com/tryton/tryton)**  

  Modular, three-tier ERP written in Python with separate packages for accounting, sales, purchasing, stock, and production. Production modules cover routing steps, work centers, and cycle tracking in detail . Strong emphasis on API stability and long-term support makes it ideal for developers building specialized solutions . GPL-3.0 licensed. Best for manufacturers and process-led companies with technical teams.



- **[metasfresh](https://github.com/metasfresh/metasfresh)**  

  Open-source ERP focused on distribution, warehousing, logistics, and supply-chain execution. Modules include product data, MRP II, order picking, traceability, and EDI workflows . Java-based with 1,900+ stars. Best for distributors and wholesalers that need deep warehouse and logistics capabilities.



- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**  

  Open-source ERP & CRM for small and medium businesses, foundations, and freelancers. Covers contacts, suppliers, invoices, orders, stocks, agenda, and accounting . PHP-based with GPL-3.0 license. Includes a JSON API for integrations. Best for smaller teams that need essential business functions without the complexity of larger suites.



- **[ADempiere](https://github.com/adempiere/adempiere)**  

  Community fork of Compiere with a strong focus on internationalization, multi-currency, and multi-lingual capabilities. Covers ERP, CRM, and supply chain management . Best for global businesses that operate across borders with complex tax and currency requirements.



- **[iDempiere](https://github.com/idempiere/idempiere)**  

  Community-powered full open-source business suite covering ERP, CRM, manufacturing, supply chain, and POS. Java-based with 665+ stars . A mature fork of ADempiere/Compiere with active development.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Aureus ERP** — Plugin-based ERP with modules for CRM, inventory, accounting, manufacturing, and purchasing. Included in feature comparisons alongside ERPNext and Odoo .

- **blueseerERP** — Free ERP and EDI solution specifically for the manufacturing community. 172 stars with recent updates .

- **OpenXE** — Free open-source ERP based on Xentral, with 86 stars and active maintenance .

- **uzERP** — Open-source business management system with 62 stars, updated through 2026 .

- **Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **ERPNext** for full-suite operations with **Frappe** for custom app development and MCP server integrations . Use **Tryton** for manufacturing-heavy processes requiring modular stability, **metasfresh** for distribution logistics, and **Odoo Community** when module breadth is the priority.
